1 · Presets

Drag the square, hover the circle. The spec says draggable and hover_scale; no per-node code.

2 · An ad, as data

A looping campaign banner: headline pops in (outBack), the badge pulses (thereAndBack), the underline draws itself (draw), every 4 seconds (loop: 4). Swap the JSON and the campaign changes — no deploy.

3 · Seek: scrub a choreography

This scene is never started. The slider calls scene.seek(t); because keyframes are a pure function of time, any position is exact — the same mechanism a scroll-driven explainer or a video exporter uses.

4 · 3D: the figure-8 Klein bottle

A space3d node naming klein8, spinning slowly (spin). Drag to orbit (orbit). Depth-banded: far lines faint and thin, near ones bold, faces covering what's behind them.

5 · A character with emotions

The character plugin with a hand-written face. Change emotion and the face swaps while the motion eases into the new gesture.

6 · Interactive: match the curve

A Brilliant-style puzzle, written entirely as JSON: two params, a live plot of exp(-a*x)*sin(b*x), two in-scene sliders, a draggable handle (control) that also sets b, a readout template {a:2}, and a "Matched" line that only shows when both are right (visible_when). No code; no eval — the formulas go through a whitelisted parser.

7 · A living graph

The graph plugin: stars repel, links pull, the layout cools and stops computing. Drag a star; drag empty space to pan; wheel or pinch to zoom; hover to light a neighbourhood; click to open.
